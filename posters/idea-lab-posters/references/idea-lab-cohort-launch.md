# Idea Lab — Cohort launch poster

## When to use
This is a **layout/style reference**, not a recurring-program recipe: the
Idea Lab × Logidoo cohort it was measured from was a one-off launch and may
never repeat under that name or with that partner. Reach for this file
whenever a new brief needs a cohort/program-launch announcement naming an
external partner and a mission statement over a photographic background —
regardless of which CONCREE program or partner is actually involved. It is
distinct from the other 4 templates in this pack by its photographic (not
flat) background.

## Adapting this template to a new, unrelated event
- Swap the program wordmark, cohort tag, accent color, and theme photo for
  the *new* launch's real partner, sector, and identity — don't default
  back to "Idea Lab" / "Logidoo" or the logistics-drone photo unless the
  new brief is genuinely another Idea Lab × Logidoo cohort.
- Keep the darkened-photo-background + bordered text panel + partner card +
  benefit-highlight composition; source a new on-theme photo for the new
  sector rather than reusing the logistics imagery.
- Re-derive the palette from the new event's own sourced material instead
  of reusing the teal/gold read below.

## Canvas & background
- 1:1 square, ~2160×2160px.
- A real, on-theme photo (related to the cohort's sector — e.g. a delivery
  drone and cargo robot for a logistics-themed cohort) fills the right two
  -thirds of the canvas, shot with shallow depth of field / soft bokeh, and
  is darkened toward a deep teal-black wash on the left so the text panel
  stays legible without a hard color block.

## Zones (top to bottom / left to right)
1. **CONCREE logo card** (top-center, ~y 0–140px): the CONCREE logo sits on
   its own small white rounded-bottom card, centered horizontally, slightly
   overlapping the top edge — the only pure-white element on the canvas.
2. **Program logo** (~y 180–560px, left-aligned): the Idea Lab wordmark
   ("iDEA" + "Lab", multi-color letters echoing the CONCREE mark's palette,
   with a small accent sparkle beside "Lab").
3. **Cohort tag** (~y 260–360px, top-right): "Cohorte N" in bold accent
   yellow, right-aligned, sitting directly over the photo area.
4. **Body copy block** (~y 620–1150px, left column, max-width ~55%): a
   short paragraph in white, mixing bold (partner names, "KPC consortium",
   "LOGIDOO") and regular weight (connecting text), introducing who is
   running the cohort and with which partner, ending with "dont le thème
   est :" followed by a bold yellow curved arrow glyph pointing down-right
   toward the headline.
5. **Theme headline** (~y 1180–1400px, left column, max-width ~65%): 1–2
   lines of bold accent-yellow display text stating the cohort's mission in
   an active, energetic phrase (e.g. "Réinventer la logistique et le
   transport avec l'IA").
6. **Partner logo card** (~y 950–1350px, right side, overlapping the photo):
   a white rounded rectangle card floating over the photo, holding the
   external partner's logo, sized generously and left-aligned within its
   card.
7. **Highlight tag** (~y 1580–1750px, right side): a rounded-rectangle
   outline badge (thin light border, transparent/dark fill) containing a
   small "#" or icon glyph plus 1–2 lines of small white text calling out a
   concrete benefit (e.g. "Opportunité de subvention d'amorçage pour les
   lauréats").
8. **Footer partner strip** (~y 2000–2160px): a white rounded-top band
   holding institutional/funder logos, evenly spaced, centered as a group.

## Color palette (approximate — verify against source PNG for exact hex)
- Background: photo, darkened with a teal-black wash, ~`#0C1F1D` overlay on
  the left two-thirds.
- Body copy: white `#ffffff`, with bold spans in white (no separate color —
  weight alone signals emphasis here).
- Cohort tag & theme headline: warm accent yellow/gold, ~`#E8A33D`.
- Program logo letterforms: multi-color, echoing CONCREE's own mark (white,
  green, coral/red, orange).
- Partner card & CONCREE logo card: white `#ffffff`.
- Highlight tag border: pale, low-opacity white/gold outline.

## Typography
- Body paragraph: regular weight with selective bold spans for named
  entities (partner names) — do not bold the connective French text around
  them.
- Theme headline: extra-bold, 1–2 short lines, left-aligned, the largest
  accent-colored text on the poster — this is the single takeaway, keep it
  to one idea.
- Cohort tag & highlight tag: bold, compact, small.

## Content slots (fill these, never invent values)
- `{{concree_logo}}`, `{{program_logo}}` (Idea Lab wordmark)
- `{{cohort_number}}` (e.g. "Cohorte 1")
- `{{running_partner}}` (e.g. "KPC consortium"), `{{external_partner_name}}`
  (e.g. "LOGIDOO") + one-line description of that partner
- `{{cohort_theme_statement}}` — a short, energetic mission phrase
- `{{theme_photo}}` — a real, on-topic photo (sector-relevant); never a
  generic stock or generated illustration standing in for the cohort's
  actual sector
- `{{external_partner_logo}}`
- `{{benefit_highlight}}` — one concrete, real benefit/opportunity line
- `{{footer_partner_logos[]}}`

## Do / Don't
- Do keep the photo background's dark wash strong enough on the left that
  white body text stays at ≥4.5:1 contrast; if the theme photo is naturally
  light, darken the overlay further rather than changing the text color.
- Do reserve the accent yellow for the cohort tag, theme headline, and
  small icon accents only — it is not a background or card color in this
  template.
- Don't flatten the background to a solid color; the photographic
  treatment (with bokeh/blur) is this template's signature, unlike the
  other 4 flat/textured templates in this pack.
- Don't invent a partner or benefit line; if the cohort's real partner or
  incentive isn't provided, leave an explicit placeholder and ask rather
  than filling in a plausible-sounding one.
