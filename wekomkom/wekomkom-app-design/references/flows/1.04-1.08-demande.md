# The request chain: 1.04 → 1.08

Five parcours that follow one request end to end: discover an opportunity (1.04),
prepare it with Adia (1.05), keep what Wekomkom knows up to date (1.06), share and send
(1.07), follow the answer (1.08). Running example: the **prêt équipement** of the
Mutuelle Jappo for a moulin (and a séchoir), plus an offer to the boutique Saveurs du
Saloum and a training at the Centre d'appui de Kaffrine.

> The example screens in this skill named « 1.04-D01…D04 » (examples/) were drawn
> before this fiche existed. Their codes collide with page 22 (1.04-D01 is « Pour toi »).
> Treat them as explorations; for real work start from the screens listed here.

## 1.04 Découvrir une opportunité

Section `463:35171` (22 screens) · working page `191:27` (Guide `198:1957`).

Rules: the fiche reads in **three levels** (l'essentiel → ce qu'il faut → tout savoir).
A status is about **one opportunity, never the person**. Missing information stays « à
éclaircir » and is solved by a question, not a refusal. Sharing with a relative sends
the opportunity's page, never the person's information (a light web page readable
without an account, her first name only if she chooses — D09, D16, D17). A closed or
« pas encore » opportunity always proposes something open now. All categories open at
launch; an empty category says so and offers to warn her (DS4). Trust has levels:
structure verified by CONCREE, opportunities spot-checked by the team or the AI; each
fiche says who checked it and when; it closes by itself at its deadline; a subscription
can speed up a check, never buy it.

## 1.05 Préparer une demande avec Adia

Section `463:35710` (23 screens) · working page `202:27` (Guide `219:1536`).

Rules: Adia never re-asks what she knows; the conversation's **subject stays pinned** at
the top (Conversation/Sujet, « Changer »). **One question per message, 2–4 ready
answers**; she can always write or speak instead. A received document is not a verified
document: Adia checks the photo, not the content, and says so. A missing document always
leads to a concrete action and a person who can help (Fatou / relais). Nothing is sent
without the entrepreneur's consent; the mutuelle or the boutique decides afterwards.
**Each structure gives its own document list** (usual list adapted per opportunity —
« Liste donnée par la mutuelle »); Adia never adds a document. Document levels: reçu →
vérifié par l'IA (sharp, complete photo; the AI can be wrong) → vérification humaine
demandée → vérifié par une personne nommée. Contrast: green text #357A11, orange text
#A15C00 (applied to the variables).

## 1.06 Ma préparation

Section `463:36266` (10 screens) · working page `254:27` (Guide `259:3114`).

Rules: **no progress bar, no « 3 sujets sur 5 »**: show the next useful thing, nothing
more. Each line says where it comes from: dit par toi · reçu · vérifié · à éclaircir.
A correction is dated; the old value stays in the history (6 months, then « Voir tout »);
**a request already sent does not change**. Old information triggers one targeted
question, not the whole questionnaire. « Je ne sais pas » leaves the line à éclaircir —
never a no. A document stays visible to her alone until she ticks it in a request.
Validity: sales and statements 3 months, ID until it expires, NINEA / registre 12 months
(a stricter opportunity rule wins). A paid option is possible only if cost, duration,
place and organiser are written before opening, and never before a useful free option.
« Vérifié » always says by whom (a structure, a relais, or Wekomkom's AI).

## 1.07 Partager et envoyer

Section `463:36517` (11 screens) · working page `223:27` (Guide `227:814`).

Rules: nothing leaves without a gesture from the entrepreneur, **on the screen where she
sees everything that leaves**. Information is proposed ticked, **documents never**; she
can untick anything. The recipient is always named, with what it will do next (it
decides). What left is kept as is; a later correction does not change it. A failed send
says clearly: nothing left, nothing lost. A confirmation appears only if something is
off (e.g. no document ticked). One channel per recipient: actor view for a structure
with an account, PDF by e-mail for a structure without, paper through a relais on
request — S01 and O01 say the channel. The phone number is pre-ticked for a verified
financer or support structure, **not for a buyer**; Wekomkom's messaging lets people
write without showing their number. A request can be withdrawn until there is an answer
(1.08-R11).

## 1.08 Suivre ses démarches

Section `463:36850` (17 screens) · working page `231:27` (Guide `239:2058`).

Rules: **a wait is neither a yes nor a no** — say it every time. The entrepreneur says
herself what she was answered; it is marked « dit par toi ». An agreement is not yet the
money received; an interest is not yet a sale. After a refusal Adia asks the reason
before proposing a next step (the reason is never guessed). No reminder leaves by
itself: Adia prepares, she sends. Each structure shows its usual delay; default 10
working days for a financer, 5 for a support structure or a buyer; Adia proposes a
reminder two days after (R01). The actor records its answer in its view (3.03); a
message never replaces this tracking. Two contradicting answers both stay, dated; Adia
helps clarify (R13). Her confirmation is enough; impact figures distinguish « confirmée
par les deux » and « par l'entrepreneure seule »; the actor can contest for 15 days.
Withdrawal possible before any answer (R11, R12).
