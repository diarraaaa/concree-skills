## What the flow is for

Aïssatou has nothing precise to do. Explorer shows what exists around her: who can help
nearby (actor map), the podcast of the week (Komkom News), structures presenting
themselves in video (Spotlight), the number not to miss (Komkom Insights), events.
Nothing she looks at is shown to a structure; an empty search says nothing about her.
A second persona (a Dakar founder building an AI app for accounting firms) checks that
Insights works outside agri-food.

## Rules

- The map opens centred on her, in a circle holding **at least five useful places** (verified structures, open opportunities, events in the next 30 days), radius between 5 and 150 km (90 km around Koungheul). Otherwise the closest with their distance (A07). She can zoom and move, then come back « autour de moi » in one gesture.
- The three-line button switches to a list, nearest first; filters stay in the chips.
- Route shown in the app, then opened in Google Maps; her position stays on her phone. OpenStreetMap background. Offline: the 30 nearest places and the last area seen (A06).
- No national total: a number around the person (X00, A02).
- A search with no result says what is missing **in the offer**, never what is missing in the person (X03).
- Komkom News keeps the accueil display, story player; the episode page has a sommaire linked to the pages concerned; the transcript keeps the spoken words while the UI stays in « tu » (K04).
- Spotlight: one-minute vertical videos, face camera, close-up, daylight, no music, French subtitles (and Wolof if Wolof is spoken), nearest first; green outline = not yet seen. Contacting a structure goes through sending her card, with her consent. Not paid at launch.
- Insights: **one number** a week, chosen from what she told Adia (activity, city, what she looks for), with its source; Adia helps to talk about it without deciding. « Ce que tu as dit à Adia » shown above the numbers, editable.
- The content team publishes News and Insights; structures propose Spotlight and fiches; CONCREE checks before publication; a reported error is fixed within 48 working hours.
- Final pass: events open on a calendar; the Zone géographique chip sits at the top (9.03).
