## What the flow is for

Aïssatou shows her card, understands what the person who scans it will see, stays in
control (choose, cut, pause), then says whether the meeting happened. She also scans a
structure's code herself, sees what a buyer would see of her, finds who saw her card,
and changes her code when she loses her carte en main.

## Rules

- **Definition of a successful connection** (adopted by management, Oct. 2026): the actor proposes a next step (meeting, documents to bring, contact), the entrepreneur accepts it, then she confirms it happened.
- Scanning a structure shows the structure nothing about her. She decides afterwards to show her card. The scanned structure only learns an anonymous total (Q02).
- A structure not yet verified is flagged and « ne rien montrer » comes first. CONCREE verifies each structure (legal existence, contact, type); an unverified one sees nothing private.
- Each type of actor sees only what is ticked for it. A buyer never sees the financing request or the documents (V01, V02). A financer preview exists (A04b).
- Every scan is visible to her, with what was shown and what was not. Scan history kept 12 months; each line can be erased (H01, H02).
- One card, two codes: losing the carte en main does not cut the card in the phone (L02, L03).
- Aïssatou lives in Koungheul on every page. Mutuelle Jappo, Mariama Ndiaye, Ndar Distribution, Baobab Négoce are fictional.
