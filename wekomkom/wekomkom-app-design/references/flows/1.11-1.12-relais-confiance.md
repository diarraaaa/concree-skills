## 1.11 Parler à quelqu'un : les relais

Section `463:37770` (16 screens) · working pages `357:27` « 11 — Relais humain » (Guide
`360:15918`) and `386:16544` « 15 — Relais » (Guide `433:23398`).

Rules:
- « Je préfère parler à quelqu'un » is possible at any time, next to Adia's answers. Adia does not hold the person back.
- A relais is attached to an institution of her territory (chambre de commerce, Espace Sénégal Services), verified by CONCREE. The profile shows: attachment, zone, languages, availability, verification status, services and prices, « Envoyer un message » (R01). What the relais does **and does not do**.
- A service is free, covered by a structure or a programme, or paid. **The price is shown before any commitment**; no hidden fees. Reporting offers: frais non annoncés · problème avec un paiement · autre problème (R04).
- By default the relais sees first name, number and the request in one sentence. **Documents are never pre-ticked.**
- Usual delay announced (two days), not promised. At 2 days the relais' structure is warned; at 4 days she gets three choices (wait, another relais, withdraw) and CONCREE is alerted.
- A relais can prepare a request with her; she alone sends it (« Préparée avec Khady Sarr », R06). After a withdrawal the relais loses access and keeps nothing.
- No relais nearby: the CONCREE team calls back at the time she chooses, within its real slots (H05). The Relais service is only offered where a relais exists (R05).
- The démarche succeeds when the relais proposes a next step, she accepts it and confirms it happened.

## 1.12 Confiance : qui a vérifié, et comment

Section `463:38164` (11 screens) · working page `386:16545` « 16 — Confiance et modération » (Guide `433:23410`).

Rules: **« Vérifié » is never written alone.** Four provenances: structure vérifiée ·
opportunité vérifiée · vérifié par l'IA de Wekomkom · vérifié par une personne ou une
structure nommée. None is a legal or administrative certification. Document states:
reçu → vérification par l'IA en cours → vérifié par l'IA (sharp, complete photo; it
doesn't read the content yet; « L'IA peut se tromper ») → vérification humaine demandée →
vérifié par [nom], with the date → à refaire (with why). Human verification: by CONCREE
at start, then by a relais or a structure; free, covered or paid, price shown (T04).
An opportunity fiche says who published it, whether the structure is verified, who
checked the opportunity and when (U01). « Signaler une erreur » on every fiche;
correction within 48 working hours, dated and visible (U03, U04).
