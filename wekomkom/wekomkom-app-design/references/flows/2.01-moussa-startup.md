Section `463:47915` (15 screens) · working page `441:27` « 19 — Moussa, startup » (Guide `446:2792`).

Moussa Konaté co-founded **Kawral**, an agricultural startup in Dakar. He writes more
than he speaks, knows what a deck is, and looks for a fundraise, an acceleration
programme, investors. **Same app as Aïssatou** (Adia, the card, démarches, same
components and rules). What changes: the opportunities shown and what a financer sees.

Use this fiche when the persona is a startup founder; keep every rule of design-dna.md.
Copy can be a little more technical (deck, levée, ticket, traction) but stays in « tu »
and short sentences; the forbidden words still apply.

## Rules

- Arrival follows Aïssatou's; Moussa can write instead of speaking (M01, M02).
- An opportunity says why it is shown, without promising; it carries its provenance (N01).
- Adia rephrases each answer and asks if it is right. The document list is the programme's; Adia adds nothing (N02, N03).
- Before sending, nothing hidden: number pre-ticked for a verified support structure; the financial model is **not** ticked unless asked (N04). **A preselection is not a place** (N05, N06).
- At a scan a financer first sees Kawral, traction, team and amount sought; deck and financial model are shared on request, with an end date, the recipient's name on every page, and an access that can be cut (V01–V03, V06).
- A financer's refusal keeps its reason; Adia proposes verified financers at the right ticket size (V05).
- Fictional: Kawral, Teranga Agri, Sahel Seed, Club Teranga Angels, Coumba Diaw, Fonds Baobab Croissance, amounts and dates.
