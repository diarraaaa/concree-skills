# 7.01 Bureau et vue acteur, sur ordinateur — phase ultérieure

Section `463:51700` (9 screens, **1280 × 800**) · working page `365:27` « 12 — Bureau et
vue acteur » (Guide `369:979`).

**Decided on 4 Oct. 2026: later phase.** For this phase the mobile app comes first, for
entrepreneurs and actors alike. These desktop screens remain a reference for later. Only
design desktop screens when the brief explicitly asks for it.

## Layout

Three columns: **Navigation/Barre latérale** (Accueil, Ma carte, Explorer, Parler à Adia)
· main content · **Panneau Adia** on the right, always open (« Je garde le fil avec
toi »), closable when room is needed (as in Mes démarches). The « une seule chose à
faire » rule stays at the top of the accueil (« Pour aujourd'hui »). What Adia fills
appears in the centre as she writes it, marked « dit par toi » until verified.

## Rules

- The actor view needs a structure account (to know which type of structure opens the card, hence what to show). Without an account: « Cette carte ne s'ouvre pas pour toi » (A05).
- Each type of structure sees what the entrepreneur chose for it: a financer sees the request and the documents; a buyer sees the offer and what she can deliver, never the loan request.
- The structure proposes a next step from the card; the entrepreneur receives it in Wekomkom and decides.
- Kept requirements for the desktop phase: logout after 30 minutes idle, « ordinateur partagé » mode, nothing remembered in the browser, documents displayed without download, logout button always visible.
