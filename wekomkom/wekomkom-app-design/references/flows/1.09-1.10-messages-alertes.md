## 1.09 Messages

Section `463:37121` (12 screens) · working page `386:16542` « 13 — Messages » (Guide `407:2537`).

Wekomkom has its own messaging, for entrepreneurs, structures and relais. People talk
without showing their number. It **never replaces the tracking of a request**: an
official answer (information requested, agreement, refusal, money paid, meeting) is
recorded in the démarche and shows in the conversation as a separate block
(Messages/Suivi enregistré).

Rules: **no new tab** — the profile photo is the entry, with a red dot when a message is
unread; the Messages line of the profile carries the unread count. A notification never
shows the content of a message (M10). « Envoyer un message » lives on a structure's
fiche (M12), a relais' profile (4.x), an entrepreneur's actor view (3.01). One can mute a
person or report a conversation (M11).

## 1.10 Alertes et notifications

Section `463:37375` (13 screens) · working page `349:65` « 10 — Alertes et notifications » (Guide `355:700`).

Wekomkom comes to Aïssatou instead of waiting for her to open the app. Every message
says why it arrives; none shows the detail of a request, neither on the lock screen nor
in WhatsApp.

Rules:
- WhatsApp is the main channel (one official WhatsApp Business account; templates written by product, reread by a Wolof speaker, submitted to Meta). The app notification only repeats important messages. SMS only if WhatsApp fails, for an actor's answer and the day-before reminder.
- **Four kinds of messages, no more**: a structure answered · a meeting or deadline (the day before, around 20 h) · a matching opportunity (one a week at most) · the Komkom News episode (off by default).
- **Two proactive messages a week at most**, except actor answers and reminders; the rest goes into the Monday summary, sent only if there is something to say. **Quiet hours 21 h – 7 h** for everyone.
- A message never shows the content of an answer, an amount or a document (the phone may be lent).
- Stopping: one word (STOP) or two taps, without guilt. Answers stay in « Mes démarches ».
- « Ce que je t'ai envoyé » lists every message with its reason; kept 12 months, erasable.
- Voice message: human voice at launch, Adia's voice once validated; Wolof proofreading.
- The W screens show WhatsApp on her phone, outside the app: they are **not** Wekomkom screens (no Wekomkom chrome).
