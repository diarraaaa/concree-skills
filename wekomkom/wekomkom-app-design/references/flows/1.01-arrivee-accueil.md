## What the flow is for

Aïssatou opens Wekomkom for the first time, talks with Adia, then lands on her
accueil. Two variants revolve around the card: she already received a card from a
structure, or she orders her carte en main at the end of the arrival. The flow must
make her feel understood, tell her what to do today on the accueil, and make the
card meaningful.

## Rules (hypotheses + « Tranché le 4 octobre »)

- No account before the first value. The WhatsApp number is asked at the end, and she can continue without it. (Final pass: sign-up by phone **or** e-mail, with a code — E10 to E10d.)
- At most **one moment a day** on the accueil. It fires on: a matching opportunity, a deadline under 7 days, an actor's answer, a scan of her card, or three months of silence.
- A card received from a structure is enough to enter: scan it, type the 4-digit code received by WhatsApp (SMS as fallback), confirm « C'est moi »; Adia starts from what the structure already gave, marked **Reçu**. No code when the structure hands over and activates the card with the person.
- Carte virtuelle offered for one month. Then: carte en main (2 500 FCFA once, retrait free or livraison 1 000 FCFA) or carte virtuelle alone (1 000 FCFA / year). Carte en main and carte virtuelle work together, **one card, two independent codes**. Without a choice the card goes **en pause**: actors can't scan it, her information is kept. Prices are provisional and live in the « Contenu » variables. A card can be offered by a programme (K04c).
- The carte en main is nominative, made on demand by a CONCREE provider, then left with a territorial partner or collected at CONCREE (K01b, K02, K05c).
- Final pass: E01 is an immersive entry screen; the accueil shows ranked real opportunities with the jauge de préparation (see README).
