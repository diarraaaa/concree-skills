## 1.13 Entre pairs

Section `463:38405` (5 screens) · working page `448:27` « 20 — Bailleurs et pairs » (Guide `449:25609`).

A pair is another entrepreneur: met at a fair, buying together, sharing a stand.
Rules: between pairs you see the activity and what is offered to others, **never the
financing need, the documents or the number** (P01). A proposal between pairs follows
the usual rule: accepted, then said « arrivée » by both (P02–P04). **Wekomkom takes no
money** (P03 « Acheter ensemble »).

## 1.14 Mon compte

Section `463:38523` (17 screens) · working page `436:28` « 18 — Mon compte » (Guide `439:2517`).

The account belongs to the person, not to her phone.
Rules: login with WhatsApp number + 4-digit code, SMS fallback, or the carte Komkom
(C01, C02); final pass adds e-mail (C01m, C02m) in a two-tab login. One phone at a time;
the other is logged out and she is warned (C03). Lost phone: carte en main, a relais or
a structure with the ID, or the CONCREE team (C04). Structures don't see the new number
without a new share (C05). « Qui voit mes informations »: each share with its date and
what left; withdrawing cuts access in Wekomkom, **a PDF already received can't be taken
back** (D02). What Wekomkom keeps, with a PDF copy on WhatsApp within 48 h (D03). Each
consent says what it allows and what refusing prevents; refusing one doesn't close the
rest (D04). Language: Adia speaks and listens in Wolof; screens stay in French for now
(D05). Deleting: consequences first, pause proposed, then a WhatsApp code; erased within
30 days with a way back until then; impact figures keep what happened, without a name.
