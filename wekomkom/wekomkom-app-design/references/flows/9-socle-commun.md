## 9.03 Où que tu sois : ton pays, puis plus loin — `517:31058`

Wekomkom starts from the person's country (guessed from the phone prefix or position,
always editable), then opens to West Africa, all of Africa, the world, or countries
ticked one by one. **One chip « Zone géographique »** at the top of content screens opens
the sheet « Où regarder ? »; the usual setting lives in « Pays et zones » in each role's
profile. Anything from another country says so, with its currency and an indicative
equivalent.

## 9.04 Ma carte et le QR, pour chaque rôle — `547:33739`

Profil → Ma carte → the card in large to be scanned, or scan yourself. A card attached
to a structure says who you are, your role and your structure, without mixing them. The
**carte Komkom belongs to the person** (it follows her if she changes structure); an
**agent card belongs to the structure** and stops when she leaves the team.
