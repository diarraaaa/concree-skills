# Zone 3 · Structures · vue acteur mobile

Section `463:48222` on page 22. Working pages: `114:27` (01, for 3.01), `386:16543` « 14 —
Vue acteur mobile » (Guide `426:4560`, for 3.02–3.07), `436:27` « 17 — Acheteurs » (Guide
`437:2573`, for 3.08). 3.09–3.11 exist only on page 22.

Structures have their own experience in the mobile app **from this phase**. A structure
opens its account, gets verified, receives requests and answers them to the end
(meeting, agreement, money paid), publishes and maintains its opportunities. A support
structure also manages registrations, attendance and card activation. Several people
share the structure's account. Desktop comes later (7.01).

## Rules (Règles posées, 4 Oct. 2026)

- **Verification**: CONCREE checks every structure (legal existence, contact, type; extra proofs for a financer). Until done, the structure sees nothing private and publishes nothing (O04, V04).
- **Publishing**: a verified structure publishes immediately; Wekomkom spot-checks afterwards (team or AI) (Z05, Z08). Paying never passes a check nor ranks higher. Editing a published opportunity doesn't change requests already sent; closing it early leaves open requests open (Z09–Z11). Every type of opportunity is created from an always-visible button (Z00); criteria are configurable (Z13–Z18); geographic scope per opportunity (Z12).
- **Documents**: each structure gives its list — a usual list, adapted per opportunity (Z03, Z04). The entrepreneur sees « Liste donnée par la Mutuelle Jappo ». A structure can verify a document; the document then shows its name, its structure and how it verified (Y13).
- **Structured answers**: information requested, meeting, agreement, not retained with the reason, money paid (Y04–Y07, F01–F06). **An agreement is not the money**: the structure indicates the payment, the entrepreneur confirms it, impact figures keep the difference. A meeting is proposed from the request; she accepts or proposes another time (F07, F08).
- **Privacy**: a structure sees only its own exchanges with a person (Y08). A connection confirmed by the entrepreneur alone can be contested for 15 days (Y09). After a withdrawal the structure loses access to documents and keeps the date and type of request (Y10). A WhatsApp message to a structure contains neither name nor request (N01, N02).
- **Support structures**: attendance at a training counts as a successful connection when both said it (G04, G05). A support structure activates a card on the spot, in front of the person, after seeing her ID; it keeps neither photo nor number (G06, G07).
- **Team**: the account belongs to the structure. A person who leaves is removed and loses access; her requests go back to a manager (K01–K05). Roles, rights, handover and invitation by WhatsApp (K06–K10). The entrepreneur sees the first name of the person following her request (K04).
- **Without account**: a structure receives a PDF by e-mail during the transition (V03). A bailleur sees totals, no identifiable person (V02). A chambre de commerce can attach relais and hand over cards (V01, V05–V08).
- **Buyers (3.08)**: a shop, company or wholesaler is a structure like any other. The object is a **product offer** instead of a financing request; next steps are échantillon, commande d'essai, rendez-vous, pas retenue (reason), livraison reçue (A05–A10). The entrepreneur's number is not pre-ticked for a buyer. **Wekomkom takes no money and guarantees no sale**; it keeps what was agreed, dated (A07). A delivery counts when both confirmed it; otherwise « confirmée par l'entrepreneure seule ». A purchase need is a Marché-type opportunity (B01–B03). At a scan a buyer sees what she offers and her activity, never her financing need, documents or number (B04).
- **Agents (3.09)**: a structure gives cards to its staff. With it an agent scans, records meetings and keeps contacts; the structure sees overall activity, within what each entrepreneur shares. The agent card belongs to the structure; the carte Komkom to the person.
- **Explorer (3.10)**: the same content screens as entrepreneurs, only navigation changes.
- **Insights (3.11)**: each type of structure sees what helps it decide (needs to support, requests to finance, suppliers to find). **No name without consent.**
