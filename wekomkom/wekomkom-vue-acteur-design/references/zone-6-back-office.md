# Zone 6 · Équipe de CONCREE · back-office — 6.01 Modération et décisions

The back-office is **drawn in mobile**, like the rest of this phase. Persona: Codou
Mbaye, équipe CONCREE (fictional).

## Rules

- « Vérifié » is never written alone. Four provenances: structure vérifiée · opportunité vérifiée · vérifié par l'IA de Wekomkom · vérifié par une personne ou une structure nommée. None is a legal or administrative certification.
- **Queues** (B01 « À vérifier »): filter chips Tout · Structures · Signalements · Contrôles IA · Au hasard, each with its count; each item says type, object, who/when, and the deadline (« à traiter avant le 10 oct. »). Other queues at the bottom: Pièces à vérifier · Vidéos Spotlight · Relais à vérifier · Désaccords d'argent · Cartes en main à fabriquer. A « Tous les pays » chip on top (9.03).
- AI checks: date, amount, link, duplicates, doubtful information. Spot-checks at random. Reports handled within 48 working hours. **A subscription can bring a check forward, never change its result.**
- **Every decision shows the text the structure, the relais or the entrepreneur will read** (D01, D03, D04, D06).
- A refused structure can answer with an official document; its documents are erased after 30 days (D03).
- An AI error is withdrawn and kept to improve the checks (D05).
- A report against a relais: his version first, then the decision; he doesn't know who reported (D06).
- A Spotlight video is watched before publication; a financing promise is enough to ask for a correction (D11).
- A relais is verified on identity, attachment confirmed by his institution, and signed charter (D12).
- Money disagreement: CONCREE doesn't decide who is right; it helps both explain, documents in hand; the démarche shows « Versement en discussion » and impact figures don't count it (D13).
- Cartes en main are made on demand, nominative, and handed over only in person (D14).
